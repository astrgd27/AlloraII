# Polygoetia — The Enchanted Archipelago (Reference)

Oct 18, 2026 · @Astral

## At a glance

Polygoetia is Allora's end-game realm: sixteen enchanted islands inside a ring of ancient storm, with activities from level 70 to 95. It is one realm shown as **two region screens** on the world map:

- **The Sunward Chain** (70–84) holds the harbours, the pirates, the kingdoms nearest the doors, and the University.
- **The Heart-Sea** (82–95) lies deeper inside the Ring and holds the stranger powers.

Every island has its own people and culture, and nearly every island has its own magic. It is the most magical realm in Allora. No mainland power holds anything here: what the mainland knows of the islands, it learns from what the islanders choose to sell.

The themes, as they appear in the world. The real-world sources stay out of game text, as everywhere else.

| Inspiration | In Polygoetia |
| --- | --- |
| The Tempest, with the fae | Miravel: a shipwrecked mortal mage, the Warden, keeps the mortal half of an island whose hidden half belongs to a fae court of revels. The child he raised as his daughter is not the one he brought ashore |
| Māori culture and the shogunate | Orivaka: a sacred line of Sun-Born chiefs who reign while a Spear-Regent rules, with sworn houses, carved meeting-houses, greenstone blades and hill-fort castles. It shelters the University in exchange for its protection |
| Historical Java, with whales | Puruvala: a tributary sea-empire whose temple-mountain capital stands on the back of a sleeping whale the size of an island, and whose mandala magic grows with every court that honours you |
| Samoan and Bedouin life | The Tuneri of the Wandering Shoal: titled families and their orators, fine mats as wealth, a sacred guest-law, and great caravans of crab-drawn sand-sleds across a shoal that is half desert and half sea |
| Polynesian voyaging | Oloa's Wayfinders, Ruhiva's blood-ink kin, and Ascara, a civic republic of cliff-clans who all fly |
| Indonesian spice kingdoms | Kelumbra, the Lamplit Kingdom of spice terraces, gong-halls and shadow-plays, and Mother Smoke, its holy volcano |
| Elemental magic | The University of the Four at Halcyra, famous across Allora, teaching Pyromancy, Hydromancy, Geomancy and Aeromancy, first alone and then combined |
| The pirates' golden age | The Articled Court at Freewater, a pirate republic at war with the wing-riders of Ascara |
| Magical weather | The Weathers, elemental fronts that cross the islands each day. Only the Tempest Callers of Ulumere can call them, through shell music |

There are three new disciplines (Aeromancy, Hydromancy and Mentalism), a new performance skill (Shell-Song), and twelve island schools. Like the rest of Allora, the archipelago is static: the powers hold each other in balance, and the Ring is never lifted.

## Where it lies and how you get there

Polygoetia fills the **Warm Sea**, south of Thoolma's Raizon coast and east of Qoul's Sealands, and becomes the fifth land on the world map. There are two doors through the Ring. Each is held open by Tempest Callers singing from the ships' bows.

| Door | Sails from | Days at sea | Fare | Lands at |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Southern Gap** | Plandor's harbour, down the Raizon coast | 7 | 600 pf | Brightwater Key |
| **The Pearl Road** | Dilmun in the Sealands, east along the pearl banks | 5 | 500 pf | Brightwater Key |

These times fit the routes already in the game: Marre to Plandor is 7 days, Dilmun to Plandor 4, and Tarsena to Port Abzu 6.

**The permit is one simple step,** like the Sea-Seal. There are two ways to get it:

- **The Articles of Safe Passage**, sold at the Articled Court's consulates in Plandor and Dilmun: twelve skills at level 70 and 600 pf. The first passage is included.
- **A letter of admission to the University at Halcyra**, which counts as a passage: any one element discipline at 65 and the University's fee. This is the scholar's way in, and the reason students from every land make the crossing.

Inside the realm, ferries run between the islands of each region screen. Crossing between the two screens means a Caller-piloted boat through the inner currents, about a day. A few places can be reached only in certain Weathers: the Drowned Market in the Long Ebb, and the Eye-Rock in the Eye.

## The Ring and the retcon

Nobody raised the Ring, and nobody can lower it. It is **the Long Storm**, a belt of weather that has circled the Warm Sea since before any history. Wind, water, fire-lightning and flying sand churn in it without end. The mainland has always bought the islands' goods without seeing the islands: everything passes through two doors and the hands of islanders who do not explain themselves.

Each people explains it differently:

- **The Tempest Callers:** the Ring is the sea holding its breath. Their shell-choirs learned, generations ago, to sing two doors into it. They sing them open still, from ships' bows and once a year from the Eye-Rock.
- **The University:** it is the four elements in permanent argument, a quarrel too large to settle.
- **Puruvala:** the Ring is the Great Whale's dream of the edge of the world, and it will end when the Whale wakes. The Whale has not woken.
- **Thoolman natural philosophers:** the Hum, which deepens towards the south, changes form at the shore. Under rock it is a sound; over open water it blows.

The Ring is part of what makes the islands high-magic: the elements are so close to the surface here that children light lamps by asking.

Each island is one game location, with its own people, culture and sub-locations. The Sunward Chain faces the doors. The Heart-Sea lies inside, upwind, and gets each day's Weather first.

## Powers and politics

Four great powers hold the balance: Orivaka, Puruvala, the Articled Court and the Tempest Callers. The smaller islands each lean on one of them. Every power is a faction with standing, and its quarrels supply the region's work.

| Power | Seat | Ruled by | Wants | Its lever |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Kingdom of Orivaka** | Orivaka | The Sun-Born reign; the Spear-Regent rules, through sworn houses of hill-fort lords | To stay unconquered, and to keep the University as its shield | Its sworn warriors and war-canoes, and the University's mages on its walls |
| **The University of the Four** | Halcyra, on Orivaka | A Rector and the three college masters, self-governing under the Charter of the Four | Students, specimens, and freedom to teach | Every power's mages trained there, and owe it |
| **The Whale-Mandala of Puruvala** | Puruvala | The Rider of the Deep and a court of poets and admirals | Every island inside its mandala of tribute, at least on paper | Whale-barges, a tributary fleet, and a magic that grows with every court it binds |
| **The Articled Court** | Freewater | A Commodore elected each year by the captains | Every prize at sea tried in its court, and its cut | Ships, and the threat of them |
| **The Tempest Callers** | Ulumere | The Great Choir: the eldest singers, mostly women | To keep the doors theirs and the weather theirs alone | Without them, nobody passes the Ring |
| **The Wing Republic of Ascara** | Ascara | The Gathering of everyone who has flown, and a Wing-Marshal it elects every three years | The pirates driven from the Sunward lanes | Fire-jar bombing from the air, which no ship can answer |
| **The Lamplit Kingdom** | Kelumbra | The Lamp-Queen, through her plays | Its spice monopoly kept | Only Kelumbra grows nutmeg and cloves |
| **The Tuneri families** | The Wandering Shoal | Titled family heads, speaking through orators | Open routes, and the guest-law honoured everywhere | Their caravans and flotillas carry most of the Sunward Chain's goods |
| **The Harbour-Mothers** | Brightwater Key | A council of market matriarchs | Fair weights, and every ship paying harbour-dues | They own the market every power must use |
| **Oloa's canoe-houses** | Oloa | A council of navigator-chiefs | Free sea-roads | They can cross between the screens without Callers |
| **The Kin of Ruhiva** | Ruhiva | Feuding kin-chiefs bound by blood-ink | Honour, and their young back from the pirate ships | The best marines in the islands, for hire |
| **The Raisers' Guild** | Stonerise | Guild-masters by contract | To sell new land to whoever pays | They can make land, and land settles arguments |
| **Miravel** | Miravel | The Warden over the mortal half; the Glimmering Court over the hidden half | The Warden: his true daughter back. The Court: guests, and revels without end | Storms the fae raise to bring ships in, and a court no one finds without leave |
| **Hushwater** | Hushwater | Nobody speaks; the eldest mind decides | Quiet | The islands' mediators: no one lies in the still lagoon |

**Orivaka and the Charter of the Four.** Halcyra stands on Orivaka's southern shore, and it governs itself inside its walls under the Charter. In return, its masters stand on Orivaka's walls whenever a greater power comes: the Court's raiders, Puruvala's tribute-fleets, Ascara's wings in a bad year. The Spear-Regent's young lords want the University's fire for war. The University answers that the Charter says *protection*, and that is all it will give. Every few years the question nearly breaks the Charter, and every time it holds.

**Puruvala's mandala.** In theory, every island is a ring in the Whale-Mandala and owes tribute. In practice, each sends a token: a pearl, a song, a single nutmeg. Puruvala sends back gifts worth more, because binding the islands in rings matters more to it than wealth. Only Orivaka sends nothing, and only Orivaka has never been visited by a tribute-fleet with real cannon.

**The Long Raid.** Ascara's wing-riders bomb pirate ships in the Sunward lanes, and the Court's ships raid Ascaran fishing fleets. Neither can win: wings cannot hold a harbour, and ships cannot reach a cliff. The player can fly for Ascara or sail for the Court, but not both.

**The Warden of Miravel.** He is a mortal mage, shipwrecked on Miravel long ago with his infant daughter. He struck a bargain with the Glimmering Court: the mortal half of the island to keep, and his skill in their service. The price was his daughter, taken under the hill. The fae left him one of their own children in her place. He raised her as his heir, and she is the island's princess, beloved and not quite human. He knows. She may not. He spends his life looking for a way to win his true daughter back without breaking a fae bargain, and he never will.

**New land.** Stonerise can raise an islet from a reef in a season, and every new island starts a dispute. The Court claims all new reefs, Puruvala adds each islet to its mandala, and Kelumbra claims anything within sight of Mother Smoke. A late player home could be an islet of the player's own, raised by contract.

## The Weathers

Each region screen has one Weather a day. The wind blows from the Heart-Sea towards the Sunward Chain, so today's Weather in the Heart-Sea is usually tomorrow's in the Sunward Chain, and a player who reads it can plan a day ahead. The Weathers do here what the Hum does in the East: they make each day different and favour some magic over other magic.

| Weather | Element | Feel | Magic | Life and work |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Gold Calm** | none | Flat sea, hard light | All schools steady | Diving and fishing better; sailing slow; Ascara grounded (no updraughts). The most common Weather |
| **Salt Gale** | Air | A dry hard wind full of spray | Aeromancy ×1.4 | Sailing fast; Ascara's wings fly far; more mishaps at sea |
| **Green Rain** | Water | Warm, heavy rain smelling of leaves | Hydromancy ×1.4 | Plots grow a day faster; fevers more likely |
| **Glass Fog** | Water and mind | A bright fog full of reflections | Illusion and Mentalism ×1.4 | Miravel and some hidden places become easier to find; navigation hard |
| **Ember Squall** | Fire | Warm ash-rain from Mother Smoke | Pyromancy ×1.4; water workings weak | Smithing and glass better; crops scorched |
| **The Long Ebb** | Earth | The sea draws far out and stays out | Geomancy and Crystal Magic ×1.4 | Reefs can be walked; the Wandering Shoal's caravans cross the dry sands |
| **Singing Gale** *(rare)* | Air and voice | A wind that carries sound for miles | Shell-Song doubled; spoken workings reach further | Performance pays double; secrets travel |
| **The Eye** *(rarest)* | All four | Dead calm under a clear circle of sky | Every elemental working ×1.5; Fourfold Quintessence workings possible anywhere | The Ring thins, and the Eye-Rock can be reached |

**Only the Callers make weather.** The University teaches the four elements but never weather. It calls weather "a quarrel between elements, not an element" and leaves it to Ulumere under an old agreement, the Concord of the Falls. A Halcyra student caught trying is sent down. Tempest Callers can call a Weather to one island for a day, and at the top of the art hold one for a season. The Articled Court, Ascara and the farmers of Kelumbra all buy the Callers' weather, so the Callers are rich and careful.

**Forecasting.** A new skill, Weather-Lore (landcraft), forecasts one day ahead at 40, three at 70 and a week at 90. Oloa's navigators and the Callers are the best at it.
