# Dellian · culture guide

**Status: approved by the owner, 2026-11-13.** See [the overview](the-seven.md) for how to read this.

## In one line

A small white city-state on a lake, ruled by the Church of Dell, that keeps the peace of the Seven by being neutral, careful and needed.

> “The smallest of the three great powers in land and the greatest in influence.” — `realm_dellian`

> “Dellian has almost no army and needs none: everyone needs it more than they would gain by taking it.” — `realm_dellian`

## Names

Dellian draws its people from every realm, and its names show it.

**People in canon:** Casimir Montfalco, Agathe Spindler, Ilse von Arnhold, Rodrigo de Alcaraz, Benvenuto Mocenni, Jost Rennwald, Aldo Ferrant, Mother Ysolde, Lady Brunhild Vollrath (a hostage), Brother Anselm, Isaure Vellin, Matthias Grau, Odile Fenn. One is known only by office: the Lamplighter.

**Places in canon:** Dellmont, Lake Lumen, Vescova, Ceraluce, the Lumen Reedmarsh, the Lantern Palace, the Street of Legations, the Strongrooms, the Villa of the Hostages, the Hospice of Saint Wendel, the Hospice of Saint Ambros.

**Patterns:**
- **The clergy come from everywhere.** The four Lamp-Prelates carry an Eintennish name (von Arnhold), a Luggish one (Spindler), a Sarlonese one (de Alcaraz) and a Marrean one (Mocenni), and each leads the circle "close to" that realm.
- **Dellian's own people** have soft, short names: Isaure Vellin, Odile Fenn, Matthias Grau, Aldo Ferrant.
- **Religious** are `Brother`, `Mother` + a given name only: Brother Anselm, Mother Ysolde.
- **Places** are named for light: *Lumen*, *Cera-luce*, the Lantern Palace, the City of Soft Light. Others are named for what they are for: the Street of Legations, the Strongrooms, the Villa of the Hostages.
- **Hospices** are named for saints: Saint Wendel, Saint Ambros.

## Titles and ranks

- The ruler is the **Arch-Bishop** of the Church of Dell. Under him, the **Lamp-Prelates of the Circle**.

> “for twenty years now the formidable Casimir Montfalco” — `realm_dellian`

- **The Curia:** Lamp-Bearer of the Curia, Secretary to a Prelate, Chaplain of the Circle, Monsignor of the Lamp.
- **Service of the Diet:** Courier, Clerk, Notary, Interpreter, Senior Mediator.
- **The Lamp-Guard:** Recruit, Halberdier, Sergeant of the Lamps, Lieutenant of the Guard, under a Captain.
- **The Quiet Office:** Listener, Watcher, Handler, Lamp of the Quiet Office, under a Master known as the Lamplighter.
- **Dell's faithful** rise from Listener to Voice of the Diet.
- Other offices: Keeper of the Strongrooms, Abbess of the Cloisters, Hostage of the Diet.

## Faiths

**Dell of the Soft Light** is the state. No other faith has a site in Dellian.

> “A white city on a lake at the heart of the Seven, ruled by the Arch-Bishop of Dell, Casimir Montfalco.” — `dellian_city`

The guild that serves it is the Chandlers of Ceraluce: "wax-chandlers, lamp-glass blowers and oil-pressers who keep the lamps of Dell burning".

## Food and drink

Canon dishes: **Cloister Herb-Loaf**, **Lake Perch in Sorrel**, **Cloister Liqueur**, **Lumen White**.

> “herons, wildfowl, eels and the punt-men who know every channel” — `reedmarsh`

> “Colza presses by the water, and vines climbing the slope behind.” — `ceraluce/oilmill`

The lake and the cloisters feed the city: fish, herbs, white wine, and good plain cooking.

## Dress

Canon gives one livery, twice.

> “Halberds, yellow-and-white livery, and very good soup.” — `dellian_palace/barracks`

**Lamp-Guard Livery** is a canon garment.

**Canon is silent** on clerical and civil dress. **Proposed:** white and pale yellow as the city's colours, a small lamp as the sign of office, and legations dressing in their own realm's fashion, so that the Street of Legations is the one place every costume of the Seven is seen together.

## Oaths and sayings

One oath is canon, the Lamp-Guard's:

> “who take an oath to Dell to defend the Diet, the palace and the Strongrooms and to draw steel only when the lamps are threatened” — `lore_lampguard`

**Canon is silent** on everyday oaths. **Proposed:** oaths by the lamp ("as the lamp burns", "in the soft light"), and a diplomatic habit of saying less than one means.

## What people value

- **Neutrality, visibly kept.**

> “He presides over the Diet from his chair a careful half-step outside the circle and has never, in twenty years, been caught favouring anyone.” — `ppl_montfalco`

- **Rules that do not bend,** even for one's own.

> “she has never been known to bend a rule, including in Eintenn’s favour” — `ppl_arnhold`

- **Safe keeping.**

> “a quiet counting-house takes private deposits at a modest fee, and has never been robbed” — `strongrooms`

- **Knowing, and not using.** The Quiet Office "knows everyone's secrets so that nobody can use them".

## Rules for new names here

- First decide where the person is from. A cleric or envoy takes the naming rules of their home realm; say which realm in the entry.
- Native Dellians: soft, short given and family names.
- Religious: `Brother`, `Sister`, `Mother` + one name.
- Places: names of light (*Lumen*, *-luce*, Lamp, Lantern), or a plain name for the place's use. Do not reuse Dellmont, Lumen, Ceraluce or Vescova.
