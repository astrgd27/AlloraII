# Marre · culture guide

**Status: draft, waiting for the owner's approval.** See [the overview](the-seven.md) for how to read this.

## In one line

A republic on a hundred islands, faced with glass and mirror, ruled by a Doge and ten families, whose galleys carry spice, silk and glass to every port.

> “the City of Sea-Glass, built on a hundred islands in a turquoise lagoon, its palaces faced with sea-glass and silvered mirror” — `realm_marre`

> “Its illusionists work through mirrors, and its politics are much the same.” — `realm_marre`

## Names

**People in canon:** Ottaviano Grimassi, Piero Grimassi, Isotta Morvan, Alvise Morvan, Benedetto Loredel, Zanetta Brusà, Marin Querzi, Orsola Vianel, Ermolao Bembel, Zanetta Bon, Bastian Caro, Momolo Scarpa, Cateruccia Vio.

**Places in canon:** Marre, the Glass Isles, Selino, Morena, the Lazaretto, the Terraferma, the Piazza of the Doges, the Grand Canal Palazzi, the Rialto Exchange, the Lantern of Cape Drown, the Marble Shallows, Sunken Xonia.

**Patterns:**
- **Given names** are lagoon names, many in a familiar form: Marin, Alvise, Zanetta, Momolo, Bastian, Cateruccia, Orsola, Ermolao, Isotta.
- **Family names** are short and clipped, often ending in a consonant or a stressed vowel: Bon, Vio, Caro, Scarpa, Querzi, Vianel, Bembel, Loredel, Brusà. This clipped ending is the mark of the lagoon and sets Marre apart from Rolla.
- **A great house** is `Ca'` + the name: Ca' Grimassi, Ca' Morvan, Ca' Loredel. There are ten.
- **People are known by a byname:** the chief scryer is "the Blind Mirror".
- **Places** keep the southern form for the city's own things (Piazza, Palazzi, Lazaretto, Terraferma, Fondaco) and plain speech for the sea around it (the Glass Isles, the Marble Shallows, Cape Drown).

## Titles and ranks

- The **Doge**, elected for life from the Ten Families; the heads of the houses; the Great Council; the Procurators.

> “The Doge is elected for life from one of the Ten Families and rules, with their councils, a colonial empire of islands and trading posts across the southern sea.” — `realm_marre`

- **The Cerdans (investigators):** Informer, Constable, Inquirer, Mirror-Scryer, Capo's Deputy, under the **Capo**.
- **The Glass Isles:** Furnace Boy, Glass Apprentice, Mirror-Silverer, Master Mirror-Maker.
- **The Lazaretto:** Corpse-Bearer, Orderly, Plague-Doctor, Surgeon, Physician, under a **Prior**.
- **The Morena Fondaco:** Writer, Under-Factor, Factor of Morena, Bailo to Thoolma.
- **The Lilac Loop (Selino's underworld):** Loop-Runner, Door-Bravo, Croupier, Fixer, Loop-Lord.
- **Service below stairs:** Scullery Maid, Housemaid, Parlour Maid, Lady's Maid, Head Housekeeper.
- **Dolma's faithful** rise as citizens do: Lagoon-Child, Citizen, Lion-Keeper, Procurator, Serene Counsellor, Daughter of the Matron.

## Faiths

- **Dolma, the Serene Matron**, is Marre itself made divine, worshipped nowhere else.

> “A chapel of the glass guild where every master’s first good piece is offered to the Matron.” — `marre_isles/chapel`

- **Pecutto of the Cup** has his one temple here.

> “A modest temple down a side-canal, smelling of wine and greasepaint, with a painted cart always at the door.” — `marre_city/temple`

- **Saint Calder** has a reliquary, and an argument:

> “A gilded chapel holding bones the Marreans swear are Calder’s and the Eintennish swear are a pig’s.” — `marre_palace/reliquary`

- **Horro** has a Hall in the city.

## Food and drink

Canon dishes: **Sardines in Saor**, **Lagoon Risotto**, **Saffron Rice of the Lagoon**, **Marrean Spiced Wine**.

> “Bankers, speculators, perfumers and spice-sellers.” — `marre_city/rialto`

Fish from the lagoon, rice, and the spice the galleys bring home.

> “every autumn they come back low in the water with pepper, silk, pearls and men who have not been paid in eight months” — `lore_selino`

## Dress

**Canon is nearly silent.** It mentions silk as cargo and contraband, "a sailor's cap", and a nurse's "little bag of herbs to wear against the sickness". The Marrean abroad is "impeccably dressed" (the ambassador in Rolla).

**Proposed:** silk and glass beads as the city's own show, masks for the stage and the revel (the Teatro della Maschera and the Silvered Masque are canon names), and the Ten Families marked by a house colour. Glassmakers dress richly and cannot leave.

## Oaths and sayings

Marre's great public vow is the Doge's:

> “Every boat in Marre follows the Doge’s golden barge out to wed the sea.” — `marre_palace/feast`

**Canon is silent** on everyday oaths. **Proposed:** people swear by the lagoon and the Matron ("by the Serene", "as the lagoon keeps me"), and by their house. A Marrean promise is carefully worded, and the wording matters.

## What people value

- **Patience and being underestimated.**

> “He is very small, very old, very rich, and has outlived every rival by the simple method of being underestimated by each in turn.” — `ppl_grimassi`

- **Never losing your temper.**

> “he regards them with the patient hatred of a man who has never once lost his temper” — `ppl_piero`

- **Secrets kept, at a price.**

> “Marre’s glassmakers live in gilded captivity — honoured, rich, and forbidden on pain of death to leave” — `marre_isles`

- **Prudence as a civic virtue:** Dolma's domain is "its glass, its prudence, its councils and its lagoon".
- **Fear of the watchers.** The Doge keeps the Capo of the Cerdans "precisely because the Families fear him".

## Rules for new names here

- Given names: lagoon names, familiar forms welcome. Family names: short and clipped. If a name would sit as well in Rolla, clip it.
- Houses: `Ca'` + name. The Ten Families are a fixed number: a new great house is a large change, so ask first. Lesser houses and merchant families are free.
- Places: southern form for the city's institutions, plain speech for islands, shoals and capes. Do not reuse Grimassi, Morvan, Loredel, Selino or Morena.
- Bynames ("the Blind Mirror") suit anyone of the Cerdans or the stage.
