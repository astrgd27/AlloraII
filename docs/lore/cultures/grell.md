# Grell · culture guide

**Status: approved by the owner, 2026-11-13.** See [the overview](the-seven.md) for how to read this.

## In one line

A small mountain duchy of dark woods and long memory, where the dead are kept close and a foreign chancellor holds the seals.

> “Grell is a land of crooked half-timbered towns, onion-domed chapels, bone-vaults, dark beer and darker woods, where the winters are long and the memories longer.” — `realm_grell`

> “the year turns on the Hollow Nights, when the living wear the masks of their dead, the chapel bells toll every name, and the dead are said to walk the streets” — `realm_grell`

## Names

**People in canon:** Ottwin Perlin, Ansgar Holle, Ursel Kranach, Gundel of the Moor, Konstanze Aurach, Gebhard Uhl, Severin Moll, Old Wigand, Lenz Hauser, Walburga Pinsel, Ottilie Brand, Jakob Ried, Ruprecht Ambach. And the Sarlonese who govern: Baltasar de Ocampo, Íñigo Salcedo.

**Places in canon:** Perlinburg, the Nachtwald, Schloss Trauerfels, Uhrental, Kaltbrunn, Kohlgrund, the Hanged Man's Pass, the Nameless Charnel, the Crag of Owls.

**Patterns:**
- **Given names** are old northern-highland names, heavier than Luggen's: Ottwin, Ansgar, Gebhard, Severin, Ruprecht, Walburga, Konstanze, Ursel, Gundel.
- **Family names** are one or two syllables, often a trade or a thing: Holle, Uhl, Moll, Ried, Hauser, Pinsel, Kranach, Aurach.
- **The old and the solitary** are named by a word, not a family: Old Wigand, Gundel of the Moor.
- **Places** join highland roots, and the roots are dark or cold: *Nacht-wald* (night wood), *Trauer-fels* (mourning rock), *Kalt-brunn* (cold spring), *Kohl-grund*, *Uhren-tal*. A castle is `Schloss`; the capital is the family's `-burg`.
- **Plain-speech names are grim:** the Hanged Man's Pass, the Nameless Charnel, the Plague Terraces, the Black Firs, the Wisp-Bogs.
- **Sarlonese names** appear only among the Chancery's people.

## Titles and ranks

- The ruler is the **Duke** (the Perlin family, eleven generations). The power is the **Chancellor**, a Sarlonese envoy.

> “increasingly, by Chancellor Baltasar de Ocampo, the third Sarlonese envoy in a row to hold the seals” — `realm_grell`

- **The Lantern-Watch:** Lamplighter, Night-Watchman, Mask-Warden, Captain of the Lantern-Watch.
- **The Mourning Court:** Mourning-Page, Gentleman of the Black Chamber, Chamberlain of the Mourning Court, Keeper of the Masks.
- **The University:** Trench-Hand, Field Assistant, Cataloguer of Finds, Epigrapher, Professor of Antiquities. Also a Master of Antiquities and a Prosector of the Anatomy School.
- **The Clock-Carvers:** Apprentice Carver, Journeyman Clockmaker, Master Clockmaker, Master of the Death-Clocks.
- **The Kurhaus:** Bath-Attendant, Bath-Master, Sanatorium Nurse, Kurarzt.

## Faiths

**Gruma of the Hollow Night** is the realm's faith, kept by the Mask-Wardens.

> “A chapel whose vaults are stacked with the bones of the plague-dead, arranged into arches and chandeliers, and whose walls hold the mask of every Perlinburger who has died since.” — `grell_town/chapel`

The rule of the mask, as an apprentice gives it:

> “Walburga explains that a death-mask must be carved from memory, never from life, or the dead will not recognise it.” — `walburga`

Old belief runs under it: wisps in the forest, the Moor-Wife, the Wild Hunt.

> “In the dark of winter, the old track through the forest along which, they say, the Wild Hunt rides.” — `kohlgrund/hunt`

## Food and drink

Canon dishes: **Grellish Dark Beer**, **Cabbage & Dumpling Stew**, **Totenbrot**, **Kaltbrunn Water**.

> “Dark beer, oompah, finger-pulling and fights.” — `grell_town/krug`

Heavy, dark and warm, with one food that belongs to the dead (Totenbrot) and one cure (the spring at Kaltbrunn).

## Dress

Grell is the one realm where canon says what people wear, and it is mourning.

> “Black-hung halls, viol music, and courtiers who have not worn colours in twenty years.” — `trauerfels/court`

A **Mourning Coat** is a canon garment, and the mask is worn on the Hollow Nights.

**Canon is silent** on everyday dress outside the court. **Proposed:** dark, plain, long-wearing cloth in town; colour is noticed and a little suspect; every household keeps its masks where a guest can see them.

## Oaths and sayings

Grellish speech hedges: things are "said" to be so. This is the realm's voice.

> “they say, the Wild Hunt rides” — `kohlgrund/hunt`

**Canon is silent** on oaths. **Proposed:** people swear by their dead and by names ("on my mother's mask", "by every name the bells toll"), and never name the Grey Death lightly.

## What people value

- **Remembering.** The dead are kept, named and counted.

> “the Grellish have never quite let their dead go” — `realm_grell`

- **Discretion about them.**

> “She knows the name behind every mask in the Bone-Chapel and will tell you none of them.” — `ppl_kranach`

- **Learning that others will not touch.**

> “things nobody else teaches: the lore of the dead, the anatomy of the plague-years, the optics of wisp-lanterns” — `grell_uni`

- **Endurance,** and a quiet resentment of being governed from Valmonte.

## Rules for new names here

- Given names: old, heavy highland names. Family names: short, often a thing or a trade.
- Places: highland compounds with dark or cold roots (*-wald*, *-fels*, *-brunn*, *-grund*, *-tal*); `Schloss` for a castle. Do not reuse Trauerfels, Nachtwald, Kaltbrunn, Kohlgrund, Uhrental or Perlin.
- Offices: hyphened, and often with *Mask-*, *Lantern-* or *Mourning-*.
- Sarlonese names only for people of the Chancery.
