# Qoul · culture guide

**Status: draft, waiting for the owner's approval.** See [the Seven's overview](the-seven.md) for how to read a guide.

The old empires of clay and stars, reached by the Sea-Seal. Every realm has its own tongue and its own way of building a name, and they do not mix.

This guide gives, for each realm, its canon names, ranks, faiths, food and garments, and the naming rules that follow. The lists are copied from canon by script. It does not yet propose dress or oaths where canon is silent; that waits until a phase builds in this land.

## Raben

> “The Shepherd-King of Raben rules from Assar of the Winged Bulls, a city of glazed brick and stepped temple-towers between two great rivers” — `realm_raben`

**Sound and patterns.** Names are sentences to a god, joined by hyphens: Nabu-shar-ilani, Bel-ushezib, Ashur-eriba, Kidin-Ea. Women’s names often begin Ummi-, Amat-, Nin-, Belili-. Places are a name + what it is: Kesh of the Twenty Sluices, Etemen, the Tongue-Tower.

**People in canon:** Nabu-shar-ilani (The Shepherd-King of Raben), Ummi-abzu (Chief Keeper of the Tablet-House), Ilu-kabti (Name-Warden of the Gate), Bel-ushezib (First Star-Priest of Etemen), Nin-gubla (Mistress of the Court of Horns), Ashur-eriba (Master of the Lion-Wheels), Belili-ummi (Dream-Scribe of the House of Dreams Received), Kidin-Ea (Bull-Keeper of the Gate of Assar), Amat-Sukkal (Head-Down Gardener of the Hanging Gardens), Ezzaret of the Summit (Speaker for the Winds).

**Places in canon:** Assar of the Winged Bulls, Port Abzu, The Tablet-House, Etemen, the Tongue-Tower, The Dream-Park, Kar-Alap, the Calf-Fields, Kesh of the Twenty Sluices, The Tell of Years, The House of Dreams Received, The Hanging Gardens.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The Tablet-House: Son of the Tablet-House, Seal-Scribe, Keeper of a Stack, Reader of the Lower Stacks, Warden of the Bull-Names
- The Lion-Wheels: Groom of the Onagers, Shield-Bearer of a Car, Driver of the Hunt, Captain of Fifty Wheels, Master of the Dream-Hunt

**Faiths with a site here:** Laishma, Mother of Monsters, Parod the Storm-Chief.

**Food in canon:** Abzu Sweet-Water, Star-Beer, Dream-Dates, Sky-Dates.

**Garments in canon:** Scribe’s Reed-Case, Lion-Wheel Mantle.

**Rules for new names.** Two or three hyphened parts, one of them a god’s or lord’s name. Places as `Name of the …` or `Name, the …`. Do not reuse Assar, Abzu or Etemen.

## The Sealands

> “Where the Two Rivers die into a great salt gulf, the marsh-folk of the Sealands live on reed-islands and dive for pearls, nominally the King of Raben’s subjects and actually no one’s” — `realm_sealands`

**Sound and patterns.** Marsh names are shorter Rabene: Lu-Nanna, Lu-Abzu, Geme-Tuku, Abi-simti, Ennam. Titles follow the name: Pagir the Poler, Kesh-ara the Tide-Warden. Places are plain: the Pearl Banks, the Drifting Isles.

**People in canon:** Ezkalu, the Seventh Teacher (Last of the Fish-Cloaked Sages), Kesh-ara the Tide-Warden (Keeper of the Questions), Pagir the Poler (Ferryman of the Mirror Shallows), Ama-Ushumgal (Mother of the Drifts), Old Lu-Nanna (Singer of the Divers), Abi-simti (Pilot-Mother of Kuara), Ennam (Diver of the Stone-Weighted), Geme-Tuku (Reed-Shipwright of the Drifts), Lu-Abzu (Purifier of Dilmun), Ennuk-Tal (Pilot of the Beast).

**Places in canon:** Dilmun, The Pearl Banks, The Drifting Isles, Kuara-on-the-Beast, The Stone-Weighted Deep, The Mirror Shallows, The Isle of the Sleeping Teacher.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The Stone-Weighted: Rope-Hauler, Shell-Diver, Diver of the Deep Banks, Master of the Stones, Diver of the Coral
- The Beast-Pilots: Reed-Boat Poler, Lagoon Pilot, Gulf Pilot, Reader of Backs, Pilot-Elder of Kuara

**Food in canon:** Dilmun Spring-Water, Dilmun Bread, Fire-Split Carp, Natron-Dried Gulf Fish, Gulf Grouper over Coals, Salted Tunny Loins.

**Garments in canon:** Drift-Reed Cloak, Turtle-Shell Nose-Clip.

**Rules for new names.** `Lu-` or `Geme-` + a name, or one short name + `the` + trade. Do not reuse Dilmun or Kuara.

## The Cedar Mountains

> “North-west of Raben rise mountains clad in cedars so tall their crowns are lost in cloud” — `realm_cedars`

**Sound and patterns.** A frontier of three empires, so three kinds of name meet: Rabene (Kidin-Sin, Lugal-Kisal), Ahnari (Hor-Nakht, Wenamun) and Beth-Arumite (Yo’ash ben Ahiram). Places are plain and about the forest.

**People in canon:** Kidin-Sin (Bull-Captain of the Rabene Third), Hor-Nakht (Commander of the Angle-Tower), Yo’ash ben Ahiram (Captain of the Tent-Third), Ilshu of the Flume (First of the Flagless), Lugal-Kisal (Chief of the Woodcutter Pairs), The Resin Mother (Keeper of the Cedar Forest), Shiru (Crown-Walker of the Cloud-Cedars), Abba-El (Raft-Driver of Kur-Erin), Wenamun (Angle-Archer of Cedargate).

**Places in canon:** Cedargate, the Fort of Three Flags, The Walking Forest, The Amber Hollow, The Door of Oaths, The Long Flume, The High Place of the Girdled Cedars, The Raft-Landing at Kur-Erin, The Crown-Walk.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The Woodcutter Pairs: Watcher of a Pair, Cutter of a Pair, Pair-Leader, Feller of Giants, Chief of the Pairs
- The Truce-Watch of Cedargate: Truce-Sentry, Warden of the Common Hall, Keeper of the Rota, Captain of the Truce, Truce-Marshal of the Three Flags

**Food in canon:** Cloud-Cone Bread, Snow-Sherbet of the High Place.

**Garments in canon:** Watcher’s Hood, Flagless Cloak, Cloud-Cedar Oud.

**Rules for new names.** Decide which empire the person serves and use that realm’s rules. Places in plain speech. Do not name the Resin Mother’s children.

## Beth-Arum

> “Twelve hill-kingdoms and caravan cities bound by a covenant with a god who has no name and no image, only a Voice that speaks from inside an empty Tent” — `realm_betharum`

**Sound and patterns.** Names carry a parent: `ben` (son of), `bat` (daughter of): Hoshaiah ben Elnathan, Atarah bat Hanun. Others take a byname: Mahalath the Written. Places are a name + `of the …`: Tadmor of the Columns, Ein-Shevu of the Sworn Wells. The god has no name and no image.

**People in canon:** Malkat Shalmath (Lady of the Columns of Tadmor), Hoshaiah ben Elnathan (Warden of the Tent), Judge Atarah bat Hanun (Eldest of the Twelve Judges this Halting), Mahalath the Written (Prophetess of the Voice), Old Kedemah (Singer of the Forty Roads), King Ya’udi-El of Hamath (Hill-King of Hamath, of the Twelve Kingdoms), Hulda bat-Arum (Prophetess of the Tent), Barak ben Zimri (Caravan-Master of the Incense Roads), Ephrah (Goatwarden of the Laden).

**Places in canon:** Tadmor of the Columns, The Wandering Tent, Goren-Hadin, the Threshing-Floor of Judgement, Ein-Shevu of the Sworn Wells, Hamath of the Singing Wheels, Naioth of the Inked, The Harp-Willows of Ga’ash, The Wilderness of Sung Roads, The Groves of Hadra-Mor, Kedem-of-Refuge, The Wilderness of the Laden, Seret of the Water-Wheels.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The Caravan-Fellowships: Camel-Driver of a Fellowship, Road-Captain, Caravan-Master, Father of a Column, Strategos of the Caravans
- The Courts of the Judges: Recorder of the Floor, Advocate of the Gate, Winnower of Testimony, Elder of the Twelve Gates, Assessor to the Twelve Judges

**Faiths with a site here:** The Voice in the Tent.

**Food in canon:** Tent-Dew Wafers, Camel-Kumis, Lamb Stewed with Dates, Gazelle with Pomegranate, Fig and Pistachio Cakes, Red Lentil Stew, Chickpea Fritters, Ibex Jerky, Spiced Date Relish.

**Garments in canon:** Road-Singer’s Headcloth, Mantle of the Gate, The Unwritten Robe.

**Rules for new names.** Given name + `ben`/`bat` + parent, or + `the` + byname. Never name or picture the god. Do not reuse Tadmor, Hamath or Kedem.

## Ahnara

> “South along a long green river through the desert lies Ahnara, the kingdom of geomancers” — `realm_ahnara`

**Sound and patterns.** Short, exact names, often two parts: Khaba-Sen, Ankh-Hor, Senmut-Ka, Tuya, Ipi. The ruler is an Apex with a number: Merit-Ahn the Ninth. One person is a number: Ninety-Four. Places are named for shapes and measures.

**People in canon:** Merit-Ahn the Ninth (Apex of Ahnara), Khaba-Sen (Holder of the Chair of the Right Angle), Tuya (First Folder of the House of Folding), Ankh-Hor (Eldest Reader of the Stair), Ipi (Master of the Twelve Faces), Meryt-Ahn (Hour-Keeper of the House of Kept Hours), Senmut-Ka (Geometer of the College of Angles), Ninety-Four (Speaker of the Doubles).

**Places in canon:** Meret-Ahn, The College of Angles, The House of Folding, The Stair of Halves, The Apex’s Dodecahedron, The Triangle-Fields, The Natron Lakes, The Cataract of the Southern Angle, The Fields of the Doubles, The House of Kept Hours.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The College of Angles: Rod-Bearer of the College, Surveyor of Angles, Geomancer of the Second Rank, Architect of Solids, Holder of a Chair of the College
- The House of Folding: Natron-Washer, Linen-Folder, Reliquary-Wright, Opener of the Net, Elder Folder of the House

**Faiths with a site here:** Hetet, Who Is Exact.

**Food in canon:** Angle-Bread, Blue-Lotus Beer, The Twelve-Faced Supper.

**Garments in canon:** Pleated Ahnari Linen, Folder’s Linen Gloves, Robe of the College of Angles.

**Rules for new names.** One or two short parts, hyphened. Shape, angle and number words for places. Do not reuse Merit-Ahn or Meret-Ahn.

## The Red Land

> “East of Ahnara’s river the desert is red and endless, and across it stand the tomb-fields: the Valley of Solids, where thirty generations of Apexes lie folded in their shapes, humming faintly at dusk” — `realm_redland`

**Sound and patterns.** Ahnari names with a working byname: Nebamun the Level, Khay of the Crooked Line, Tjaru the Listener. Elders are `Old` + name. Places are counted or described: the Nine Wells, the Shadow’s Edge.

**People in canon:** Nebamun the Level (First Weigher of the Hall of Two Truths), Khay of the Crooked Line (Robber-Mistress of the Valley), Tjaru the Listener (Eldest of the Hum-Guides), Hunro (Overseer of the Pale Mines of Hu), Old Sitre (Keeper of the Empty Sphere), Amenmes (Tomb-Robber of the Crooked Camp), Tiaa (Desert-Guide of the Wells), Ipuy (Assessor of the Hall of Two Truths).

**Places in canon:** The Valley of Solids, The Hall of Two Truths, The Pale Mines of Hu, The Camp of the Crooked Line, The Nine Wells, The Oasis of the Mirror, The Shadow’s Edge.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The Hall of Two Truths: Polisher of the Beam, Clerk of the Least Solid, Advocate of the Living, Assessor of the Forty-Two, Weigher of the Hall
- The Hum-Guides: Camel-Hand, Wells-Runner, Hum-Guide, Caravan-Reader, Master of the Nine Wells

**Food in canon:** Ash-Bread of the Guides, Mirage-Wine, Supper at the Edge.

**Garments in canon:** Hum-Guide’s Robe, Crooked-Line Veil, Dusk-Cloak of the Valley.

**Rules for new names.** Ahnari name + `the` or `of the` + byname. Do not reuse Hu or the Crooked Line.

## Ashqel of the Two Thrones

> “Ashqel is two cities and two kings” — `realm_ashqel`

**Sound and patterns.** Names end in -milk, -om, -oam (Yadamilk, Yehawmilk, Ahirom, Batnoam) or are short (Abdo, Arish). Each king has a time of day as byname: the Noonday, the Midnight.

**People in canon:** Yadamilk the Noonday (The Salt King of Ashqel), Ahirom the Midnight (The Sand King of Ashqel), Tabnit-Sela (Mint-Mistress of the True Weight), Arish of the Vats (Dye-Lord of the Many-Hued Snail), Mute Abdo (First of the Dumb-Coiners), Batnoam (Sworn Ringer of the Mint), Yehawmilk (Glass-Master of Ushq), Ahotmilk (Dyer of the Unfixed Multi-Color).

**Places in canon:** Ashqel-on-the-Rock, The Causeway of Even Weight, Ushq, the Shore-City, The Dye-Houses of the Many-Hued Snail, The Murex Reefs, The Hippo-Yards.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The Mint of True Weight: Assayer’s Hand, Striker, Sworn Ringer, Die-Cutter, Mint-Master of True Weight
- The Dye-Houses: Snail-Crusher, Vat-Hand, Fixer, Colourist, Dye-Lord of the Many-Hued Snail

**Faiths with a site here:** Hiyam, the Horse of the Waves.

**Food in canon:** Ashqeli Fish-Sauce Flatbread, Gulf Fish-Sauce, Aged Fish-Sauce, Black Sauce of the Two Thrones.

**Garments in canon:** Commemorative Medallion.

**Rules for new names.** Names ending in -milk, -om or -oam, or one short name + `of the` + trade. Say which city, Rock or Shore. Do not reuse Ushq.

## Elissar of the Long Night

> “Four hundred years ago a princess of Ashqel, Elishat, fled her brother the Salt King with a ship of loyal nobles and landed on a hill above the Western Gulf” — `realm_elissar`

**Sound and patterns.** Daughter-city of Ashqel, with its sounds changed: Elishat, Arishat, Sapanit, Imilce, Bodashtart, Himilko, Gisgal. Nobles are Lord, Lady + name + a byname (the Pale).

**People in canon:** Elishat the Undying (Queen of Elissar, First of the Rephaim), Lord Magonim the Pale (Of the Hundred and Four, Author of the Treatise), Arishat, Mother of Elephants (First of the Mahouts), Gisgal the Reader (Keeper of the Library of the Long Night), Lady Sapanit (Of the Hundred and Four), Imilce (Reader of the Long Night), Bodashtart (Mahout of the Double Wall), Arsinna (Bell-Dancer of the Hundred Bells), Himilko (Envoy of the Hundred Bells).

**Places in canon:** Qartosh, the New City, The Library of the Long Night, The Stalls of the Double Wall, The Place of Bones, The Round Harbour, The Estates of Magonim.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The Mahouts of the Double Wall: Stall-Hand, Mahout, Rider of the Tower, Captain of Thirty, Mother of Elephants
- The Readers of the Long Night: Lamp-Trimmer, Copyist, Reader, Annotator, Living Catalogue

**Faiths with a site here:** The Mother at the Threshold.

**Food in canon:** Elissar Puls, Passum, Aged Passum, Passum of the Long Night.

**Rules for new names.** As Ashqel, with -at, -ce, -ko endings. Do not reuse Elishat, Magonim or Qartosh.
