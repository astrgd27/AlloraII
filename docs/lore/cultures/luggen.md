# Luggen · culture guide

**Status: draft, waiting for the owner's approval.** See [the overview](the-seven.md) for how to read this.

## In one line

A league of brick harbour-towns that ships the north's timber, fish and grain, ruled by a merchant who is elected for six years and then goes back to trade.

> “governed from the city of Luggen by a Prince elected by the merchant houses to serve six years — and then go back to trade” — `realm_luggen`

> “Luggen ships most of Allora’s lumber, pitch, furs, stockfish and grain; its Kontors stand in every port, Kesselhaven included.” — `realm_luggen`

## Names

**People in canon:** Hartwig Kolbe, Ottilie Vollrath, Lambert Fiesch, Gesche Brandt, Wendel Haak, Liesbeth Graaf, Maarten Visser, Tjalda Ostrem, Ebba Rhoon, Tjalda Wierum, Okke Brand, Konrad Spindler.

**Places in canon:** Luggen, the Pine-March, Kranzholm, the Amber Strand, Grauhafen, the Deichmarsch, Hungersand, Eisholt, the river Lugg.

**Patterns:**
- **Given names** are northern-coast names, many of them short: Okke, Ebba, Gesche, Tjalda, Wendel, Maarten, Liesbeth, Hartwig, Lambert, Ottilie.
- **Family names** are short and plain, one or two syllables: Kolbe, Brandt, Brand, Haak, Graaf, Visser, Rhoon, Fiesch. The great houses are simply **House** + the name: House Vollrath.
- **No `von`.** Luggen has merchant houses, not landed nobles. Rank shows in office, not in the name.
- **Places** join northern roots: *Kranz-holm*, *Grau-hafen*, *Deich-marsch*, *Hunger-sand*, *Eis-holt*. Plain-speech names are common for wild country: the Pine-March, the Amber Strand.
- **The Marrean touch.** Luggen learned banking from Marre and kept some of its words.

> “an albergo, as the Marrean-trained clerks still call it” — `ppl_vollrath`

## Titles and ranks

- The ruler is the **Six-Year Prince**. The fleet has an **Admiral of the League**.
- **The Kontor:** Kontor-Boy, Shipper's Clerk, Kontor Factor, Senior Factor, Master Freighter. The great houses together are the Kontor League.
- **Timber and fur:** Log-Driver, Trapper of the Pine-March, Pelt-Grader, Master of the Timber Fleet. The great owners are "timber-lords".
- **The Crossbow Companies:** Arsenal Boy, Arbalester, Marine Sergeant, Captain of Crossbows.
- **The dikes** are kept by the Rime-Watch: a **Dike-Count**, and under her **Reeves** (one is the Horn-Reeve, who blows the Surge-horn).
- **Mother of the Lights** is a title at Hungersand, held by a woman the wreckers answer to.

## Faiths

- **Horro** is Luggen's own faith: money as a living current.

> “Its bankers learned double-entry from Marre and turned it, some say, into a religion: Luggen is the mother-city of the Currentists of Horro.” — `realm_luggen`

> “The greatest of Horro’s Halls, its open court always arguing.” — `luggen_city/hall`

- **Norrae, Maiden of the River**, has a pool on the timber-rivers.

> “A deep green pool where two timber-rivers meet, kept clear of logs by the Sisters of the Ford.” — `luggen_forest/pool`

- A Dellian Lamp-Chapel stands in the city, as in every capital.

## Food and drink

The richest table in canon. Canon dishes: **Raftsman's Labskaus**, **Kranzholm Stockfish**, **Eel in Green Sauce**, **Polder Cheese** (and aged, "Old Polder, Three Winters"), **Eisholt Gingerbread**, **Luggish Genever**, **Luggish Ice-Wine**, **Frost-Market Mulled Wine**.

> “the herring fleets come in silver to the gunwales every autumn” — `kranzholm`

> “Flat pastures of black-and-white cattle and cheese-houses.” — `deichmarsch/polders`

The pattern: food is named for the place or the trade that makes it (Kranzholm, Eisholt, Polder, Raftsman's), and cold is an ingredient (ice-wine, the Frost-Market).

## Dress

Canon gives the trade, not the clothes: Luggen sells fur to every court.

> “Bales of pelts, braziers, and buyers from every court of the Seven.” — `luggen_city/furfair`

> “trappers bring in sable and marten for the fur fairs” — `luggen_forest`

**Canon is silent** on what Luggish people themselves wear. **Proposed:** fur trim as the mark of standing (sable for the houses, marten below), amber as the jewel of the coast, and heavy coats and boots for the Rime-Watch, whose reeves come in with "frost in her eyebrows".

## Oaths and sayings

**Canon is silent** on spoken oaths. What canon shows instead is that Luggen measures people by their weights and their word:

> “Hartwig Kolbe made his fortune in stockfish and his reputation by never once being caught short-weighting a barrel.” — `ppl_hartwig`

**Proposed:** oaths of fair measure ("true weight", "by the ledger", "as the current runs" for Horro's faithful), and on the dikes, oaths by the Surge.

## What people value

- **Honest weight and a clean ledger.** Reputation is commercial.
- **Office that ends.** The Prince serves six years and returns to trade; no one is meant to rise above the houses.

> “The merchant houses are determined he never will be.” — `ppl_fiesch`

- **Few words.** The north coast admires people who say little.

> “Okke Brand has blown the Surge-horn nine times and spoken, by the Watch’s best count, about four hundred words in his life.” — `lg_okke`

- **Argument in its place.** Horro's court is "always arguing": debate is a devotion.

## Rules for new names here

- Given names: short northern-coast names. Family names: one or two plain syllables. No `von`, no `de`.
- Houses: `House` + family name. Do not reuse Vollrath, Kolbe, Fiesch, Brandt or Spindler.
- Towns: northern compounds ending in *-holm*, *-hafen*, *-marsch*, *-sand*, *-holt*, or plain speech for wild places.
- Offices: hyphened compounds (Dike-Count, Horn-Reeve, Pelt-Grader).
