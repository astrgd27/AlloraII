# Rolla · culture guide

**Status: draft, waiting for the owner's approval.** See [the overview](the-seven.md) for how to read this.

## In one line

A meadow kingdom of red-roofed hill-cities, painters, wine and fairies, ruled for two child kings by a regent, with Marre's hand on its shoulder.

> “A middling kingdom of meadows, vineyards and red-roofed hill-cities, under the heavy influence of Marre.” — `realm_rolla`

> “Rolla is famous for its painters, its calcio, its wine, its Hearth-Church” — `realm_rolla`

## Names

**People in canon:** Leto and Lia (the Twin Kings), Aurelio (the Lost King), Beatrice Valcorvo, Agathe of Valdora, Matton, Fiammetta Doni, Fra Lapo, Nello Bracci, Giuditta Lanza, Mother Benedetta, Pippo Sarti. From Marre: Alvise Morvan. Of the hill: the Lady of the Hill, Fiammetta of the Rings.

**Places in canon:** Valdora, the Fairmeadows, Castelvento, Lucenza, Tenuta Valcorvo, the Hushbarrows, the Cave of the Pheasant, the Palazzo della Reggenza, the Friary of the Little Brothers.

**Patterns:**
- **Given names** are southern-hill names, bright and often with a pet form: Nello, Pippo, Lapo, Fiammetta, Giuditta, Beatrice, Aurelio.
- **Family names** end in a vowel and are short: Bracci, Lanza, Sarti, Doni.
- **Royalty use one name:** Leto, Lia, Aurelio.
- **Nobles** carry a title and the house name without a particle: Contessa Valcorvo. An estate is `Tenuta` + the house.
- **Clergy** are `Mother`, `Fra` + a given name; a high priestess is given name + `of` + her city: Agathe of Valdora.
- **Places** are hill-town compounds: *Val-dora*, *Castel-vento* (wind castle), *Lucenza*. Buildings keep the southern form: Palazzo della Reggenza.
- **Fairy country is named in plain speech,** never in the southern form: the Fairmeadows, the Hushbarrows, Under the Tallest Hill, the Lady of the Hill, the Gentry.

## Titles and ranks

- **The Twin Kings**, the **Lady Regent** (a Contessa) and her regency council; a **Marrean Ambassador** close by.

> “ruled in the names of his twin children, Leto and Lia, aged nine, by a regency council under the Lady Regent, Contessa Beatrice Valcorvo — and a great many Marrean advisers” — `realm_rolla`

- **The court:** Page of the Twins, Gentleman of the Chamber, Secretary to the Regency, Tutor to the Kings.
- **The Bottega (painters):** Garzone, Apprentice Painter, Journeyman Painter, Master of the Bottega, Court Painter. The guild is the Arte dei Pittori.
- **The Comici (players):** Stagehand, Zanni, Innamorato, Capocomico.
- **The outlaws:** Pheasant Lookout, Highwayman, Pheasant-Captain, Matton's Right Hand, under the Pheasant-King.
- **The Hearth-Church:** the High Hearth-Mother keeps the Mother-Hearth in Valdora.

## Faiths

- **Ilvara of the Two Flames**, through the Hearth-Church, has her seat here.

> “whose High Hearth-Mother, Agathe, keeps the Mother-Hearth in the capital, Valdora” — `realm_rolla`

- **Honna the Headlong** belongs to the young, ward by ward.

> “The young of the town belong to Honna the Headlong, and his shrine by the race-course is hung with ten generations of wreaths.” — `castelvento`

- **The Gentry** are not a faith, but everyone reckons with them.

> “ringed with standing stones and mushroom rings that the shepherds will not cross” — `rolla_meadows`

## Food and drink

Canon dishes: **Valdoran Ragù**, **Tajarin with Truffle**, **Castagnaccio**, **Air-Dried Mountain Ham** (aged, and "Three-Year Ham"), **Valdoran Red**, **Valcorvo Riserva**.

> “Flowers, bees, sheep and vines as far as the hills.” — `rolla_meadows/meadows`

Wine named for its house or town, ham named for its years: age and origin are the point.

## Dress

Canon dresses two people, and both dress to be seen.

> “Their king holds court on a throne of saddles under a cloak of pheasant feathers, and visitors are welcome — once.” — `cave_pheasant`

> “He is charming, patient, impeccably dressed and always reasonable” — `ppl_morvan`

**Canon is silent** on ordinary dress. **Proposed:** colour worn proudly, ward colours on race days (the Palio is ridden "for the honour of the wards"), and painters known by the pigment under their nails. Marrean silk is the court's fashion and the outlaws' plunder ("stolen Marrean silk").

## Oaths and sayings

Rolla's great story is a bargain kept.

> “Three years ago he rode out alone to the Fairmeadows at dusk, saying he had a bargain to keep, and his horse came home at dawn.” — `ppl_aurelio`

**Canon is silent** on spoken oaths. **Proposed:** people swear by the hearth and by their ward, and are careful with promises near the hills: a Rollan says "if the hill allows" where another would say "certainly", and never thanks the Gentry by name.

## What people value

- **The honour of the ward,** won by the body.

> “a bareback horse-race three times round the Campo, ridden for the honour of the wards, with no rules but one” — `castelvento`

- **Craft that takes years,** and waiting your turn at it.

> “Nello grinds lapis for the Virgin’s robe, and has never been allowed to paint with it himself.” — `nello`

- **Cheerfulness,** even in outlaws: the Pheasants are "some fifty cheerful outlaws".
- **Staying Rollan.** The Regent's Party "would keep Rolla Rollan"; every reasonable Marrean offer is watched.

## Rules for new names here

- Given names: southern-hill names, pet forms welcome. Family names: short, ending in a vowel.
- Nobles: title + house, no `de`, no `von`. Estates: `Tenuta` + house. Do not reuse Valcorvo, Morvan or Doni.
- Towns: hill-town compounds (*Val-*, *Castel-*, *Monte-*, *-enza*). Do not reuse Valdora, Castelvento or Lucenza.
- Anything of the Gentry: plain speech only, and never a personal name. They are "the Lady", "the Gentry", "the Dancer".
- Note for romance: the Twin Kings are nine. They are children in every scene.
