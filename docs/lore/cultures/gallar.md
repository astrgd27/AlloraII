# Gallar · culture guide

**Status: draft, waiting for the owner's approval.** See [the Seven's overview](the-seven.md) for how to read a guide.

The Freeholds beyond the Tyer Mountains, and the unconquered peoples of the crooked finger. Each Freehold is a house with a sigil. Names are short and plain in the Freeholds, and old-tongue in the Mearc and the Knuckle.

This guide gives, for each realm, its canon names, ranks, faiths, food and garments, and the naming rules that follow. The lists are copied from canon by script. It does not yet propose dress or oaths where canon is silent; that waits until a phase builds in this land.

## Freehold of Ripla

> “Gallar’s only port, a city on stilts where the Balm meets the ocean: walkways criss-cross above the marsh in many levels, the poor live where the spring tides flood, and Castle Trollin’s tower doubles as a lighthouse on stormy nights” — `realm_ripla`

**Sound and patterns.** Soft, short names: Maud Ellery, Brisa Lowe, Osric Penn. Elders are `Mother` or `Old Mother`. Nobles are given name + house (Rollo Doll); a cadet branch adds `of` + seat. Places are plain speech about water: the Gullet, the Salt-Pans of Wend.

**People in canon:** King Rollo Doll (King of Ripla, Head of House Doll), Maud Ellery (First Sleeper of the Court), Lady Isaut Doll of Merrow (Head of the Merrow Dolls), Magister Oriel Fane (Keeper of Trollin’s Lantern), Mother Sallow (Queen of the Drowned Stairs), Teodor Vasse (A Currentist of the Portico), Old Mother Wren (Eldest of the Nimians), Brisa Lowe (Ghostcap-Gatherer of the Ripla Fens), Osric Penn (Clerk of the Registry of the Waking Code), Marit Coyle (Harbour Pilot of the Sea-Gate), Joss Pellow (Bargeman of the Balm), Merewyn Sallow (Youngest of the Sleepers).

**Places in canon:** Ripla, The Court of the Sleepers, Castle Trollin, The Ripla Fens, The Balm Delta, The Sea-Gate & Harbour, The Salt-Pans of Wend, The Gullet, Merrow House, The Temple of Nim, Kalvenhoek, the Drained Gardens.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The Court of the Sleepers: Clerk of the Court, Advocate, Novice Sleeper, Sleeper
- The Harbour Pilots: Boat-Hand of the Pilots, Pilot’s Mate, Harbour Pilot, Sea-Gate Pilot, Master Pilot of Ripla
- The Fen-Gatherers: Fen-Picker, Fungus-Finder, Ghostcap-Gatherer, Fen-Master

**Faiths with a site here:** Norrae, Maiden of the River, Nim the Dreamer.

**Food in canon:** Stilt-Level Eel Pie, Gale Ale, Waking Tea, Ghostcap Tea, Jellied Eel.

**Garments in canon:** Advocate’s Grey, Fen Pattens, Pilot’s Oilcoat, Fen-Walker’s Waders, Fen Guide-Lantern.

**Rules for new names.** Short given name + short plain family name. Fen and water words for places. Do not reuse Doll, Trollin or Merrow.

## Fin and Drell

> “Two cities facing each other across the upper estuary of the Balm, joined by three great bridges, sharing one language and refusing to share anything else” — `realm_findrell`

**Sound and patterns.** Two cities, one tongue. Given names are gentle (Coralie, Bryony, Perrin, Lew); family names one syllable or two (Greer, Venn, Crane, Tull). Bynames stand in for names in the underworld: the Tapster, Ysmay of the Ring.

**People in canon:** Farris Chandice (Lord of Drell, Head of House Chandice), Nolan Greer (Lord of Fin, Head of House Greer), Coralie Venn (Archmagister of the Scorched School), Dame Ysenne Marle (Mistress of the Guild of Florists), Hobb Tollan (Master-Keeper of the Grand Menagerie), The Tapster (Master of the Cup-Cutters), Ysmay of the Ring (Veil-Keeper of the Bride-House), Ysabet Crane (Battle-Mage of the Scorched School), Perrin Ashby (Florist’s Journeyman of Fin), Lew Fallon (Debtor of the Old Bridge), Bryony Tull (Under-Keeper of the Grand Menagerie).

**Places in canon:** Drell, Fin, The Three Bridges, The Scorched School, The Grand Menagerie, The Arch-Gate, Castle Yollin, The Under-Drell, The Long Fields, The Bride-House of Seronia.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The Scorched School: Cinder-Novice, Adept of the Flame, Hired Flame, Flame-Captain, Magister of the Scorched School
- The Guild of Florists: Flower-Hand, Journeyman Florist, Bloom-Breeder, Master Florist
- The Cup-Cutters: Lookout, Cutpurse, Housebreaker, Cup-Master
- The Grave-Jewellers: Apprentice of the Ring, Grave-Jeweller, Master of Mourning, Ring-Keeper of the Bride-House
- The Bridge-Brokers: Runner of the Bridge-House, Go-Between, Broker of Marriages, Broker of Feuds, Master of the Bridge-House

**Faiths with a site here:** Pecutto of the Cup, Norrae, Maiden of the River, Seronia, the Dark Bride.

**Food in canon:** Drell Pepper-Sausage, Fin Petal-Cake, Old Bridge Stout.

**Garments in canon:** Florist’s Smock, Scorched Mantle, Keeper’s Gauntlets, Casagna Black Attar, Crown of Named Blooms.

**Rules for new names.** Say which city a person is of, Fin or Drell. Plain compound places (the Three Bridges, the Long Fields). Do not reuse Chandice or Yollin.

## Freehold of Gramall

> “A colossal castle-city alone in the middle of the Dorrian prairie, where you can see a storm coming a day away” — `realm_gramall`

**Sound and patterns.** Prairie names are farm words: Thresher, Hay, Straw, Dray, Hobb. Elders are `Old Nan`, `Old Mother`. A craftsman may be given name + `the` + trade (Ansel the Ploughwright). The ruling house is Brall.

**People in canon:** Tamlin Brall (Lord of Gramall), Orla Brall (Captain of the Ride), Annis Hobbe (Stud-Mistress of Brall), Corbin Lisle (Bread-Warden of the Great Granaries), Old Nan Thresher (Nine Times Wake-Queen), Jack Straw (The Hopper Nobody Can Name), Jennet Hay (Serf of Hobb’s Furrow), Hal Ferrand (Lance of the Ride), Ancel Dray (Mourner of Seronia at Gramall), Old Mother Hobb (Furrow-Elder of the Long Wake), Ansel the Ploughwright (Ploughwright of Hobb’s Furrow).

**Places in canon:** Gramall, The Dorrian Prairie, Porceps’ Hoofprint, The Brall Studs, The Great Granaries, Lancefield, Hobb’s Furrow, Caddon Market, Wyvel, Bellor’s Headstone.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The Ride: Outrider, Lance of the Ride, Banner-Lance, Captain of a Squadron, Captain of the Ride
- The Brall Studs: Stud-Groom, Horse-Breaker, Stud-Master, Master of Bloodlines
- The Great Granaries: Tally-Clerk, Granary Reeve, Steward of the Granaries, Bread-Warden’s Deputy

**Faiths with a site here:** Bellor the Lucky.

**Food in canon:** Muster-Loaf, Wake-Ale, Drovers’ Hotpot.

**Garments in canon:** Ride-Coat, Wake-Crown of Wheat.

**Rules for new names.** Family names from field, harvest and horse. Places are plain: -field, -furrow, market. Do not reuse Brall, Hobb or Lancefield.

## Freehold of Shella

> “The cold north of the Freeholds, near the source of the Balm, and Gallar’s bastion against the Witch-Queens” — `realm_shella`

**Sound and patterns.** Northern and hard: Hrodna Kell, Sigrun Vey, Sefa Holm, Ulla Fordwife. Some go by one name (Jonell). The royal house is Joix. Places are plain and cold: the White Tundra, the Pale Wood, the Frozen Fords.

**People in canon:** Namere Joix (Queen of Shella), Jonell (Prophet of Qo), Edrin Joix (Heir of Shella), Hrodna Kell (Master of the Great Hunt), Sigrun Vey (Warden of the Watch-Line), Ulla Fordwife (Keeper of the Frozen Fords), Jonell (Voice of Qo, Prophet of the White Serpent), Ivo Marrant (Marble-Cutter of Hask), Sefa Holm (Leopard-Stalker of Skarra), Pell Dunmore (Beacon-Keeper of the Watch-Line), Joss Ambry (Fur-Trader of the Frozen Fords), Edric Marlowe (Lancer of the Frozen Host).

**Places in canon:** Onore, The Balm Springs, The Quarries of Hask, Skarra, The White Tundra, The Pale Wood, The Watch-Line, The Frozen Fords, The Wild Woman’s Grove.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The Great Hunt: Tracker, Ox-Hunter, Leopard-Stalker, Master of the Hunt
- The Watch-Line: Watchman, Line-Rider, Fort-Sergeant, Warden of the Line
- The Marble-Cutters: Quarry-Hand, Marble-Cutter, Quarry-Boss, Master Mason of Onore

**Faiths with a site here:** The Greenwife, Norrae, Maiden of the River, Qo the White Serpent.

**Food in canon:** Behemoth Jerky, Birch-Sap Wine, Ox-Marrow Broth.

**Garments in canon:** Ox-Hide Cloak, Shellan Snowshoes.

**Rules for new names.** Short northern given names; one-syllable family names or a trade-name (Fordwife). Do not reuse Joix, Onore or Hask.

## Freehold of Lomma

> “The cliff-hold that faces the sky: an ancient city cut into the rock of a ravine above an offshoot of the Balm, its oldest halls carved with faces turned upward by builders no one remembers” — `realm_lomma`

**Sound and patterns.** Cliff-hold names: Hild Carrow, Aud Vellam, Tamsy Rook, Linnet Hale. Bird and stone words recur. Kings are styled given name + `of House` + name. Offices use `the`: Corvin the Sun-Warden.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The Bronze-Wrights: Bellows-Hand, Journeyman of the Colour, Edge-Wright, Master Bronze-Wright, Colour-Master
- The Lift-Men: Cage-Hand, Winch-Man, Level-Keeper, Lift-Master
- The Wing-Post: Wing-Runner, Courier of the Wing, Royal Wing, Master of the Wing-Post
- Varr’s Road-Guard: Road-Guardsman, Serjeant of the Gate, Lieutenant of the Road, Captain’s Second

## Freehold of Harrowgate

> “The Pass-Hold: a switchback fortress-town astride the one trade pass through the Tyer” — `realm_harrowgate`

**Sound and patterns.** A pass town, so names mix: Gallarian (Morwen Ash, Cael Rusk, Bram Holloway) beside Sarlonese envoys (Don Fadrique de Lerma, Inés Ferrer). Rank goes before the name: Lord, Captain, Toll-Master. The house is Vane.

**People in canon:** Lord Aumery Vane (Keyholder of Harrowgate), Ide Vane (Mistress of the Archive), Captain Brannoc Harl (Captain of the Grey Lances), Don Fadrique de Lerma (Sarlonese Ambassador to Gallar), Morwen Ash (Queen of the Tyer Runners), Toll-Master Gorm (Master of the Customs-House), Captain Mordaunt Grieve (Captain of the Grim Hawks), Inés Ferrer (Clerk of Sarlon’s Embassy-House), Cael Rusk (Runner of the Tyer), Elsabet Wray (Grey Lance on Contract), Corva Venn (Lieutenant of the Grim Hawks), Bram Holloway (Sergeant-at-Drill of the Grim Hawks).

**Places in canon:** Harrowgate, The Archive of House Vane, Sarlon’s Embassy-House, Foot of the Hag’s Saddle, Varnholt, The Muster Field, The Monastery of the Storm, The Necropolis of the First Keels, Hawk’s Rest.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The Archive of House Vane: Reader of the Archive, Copyist of Charters, Genealogist, Keeper of Lines
- The Grey Lances: Grey Lance, Corporal of Lances, Sergeant of the Company, Lieutenant to Captain Harl, Captain of a Free Company
- The Customs-House: Weigher, Toll-Clerk, Tariff-Reader, Under-Master of the Toll
- The Saddle-Guides: Rope-Porter, Icefall Guide, Saddle-Guide, Master of the Saddle
- The Grim Hawks: Recruit, Hawk, Corporal of the Mews, Sergeant-at-Drill, Lieutenant of the Grim Hawks

**Faiths with a site here:** Orthun Stormfather.

**Food in canon:** Tyer Brandy, Pass-Bread.

**Garments in canon:** Key-Badge of Vane, Grey Lance Cloak, Puzzle Ring.

**Rules for new names.** Use Gallarian names unless the person is from over the pass; then use that realm’s guide. Do not reuse Vane, Varnholt or Harl.

## The Mearc

> “The southern half of the crooked finger: land of the Khilns of the bogs, the Molster of the herring coast and the Praen of the hill-forests, the last people of Gallar the Sillenians never conquered” — `realm_mearc`

**Sound and patterns.** The old tongue. Given names keep its letters (Sæthryth, Wynflæd, Hæringhythe). People are named by father (`Osgar’s-son`, `Cola’s-son`), by byname (Gull-Eye, Holdfast, Smoke-Hand, Reedfoot) or by craft (the Carver, the Scop). Places end in -hall, -hythe, -holt, -stead, -loch.

**People in canon:** Hereward Osgar’s-son (Ealdor of the Praen, Lord of Eorlhall), Mother Sæthryth (Ealdor of the Khilns), Brand Gull-Eye (Ealdor of the Molster), Wynflæd the Carver (Eldest of the Bone-Wrights), Cuthwine the Scop (Riddle-Master of Eorlhall), Leofric Holdfast (Warden of the Neck), Osgyth Smoke-Hand (Herring-Wife of Hæringhythe), Swithun Cola’s-son (Bonecarver of the Praen), Brytha Reedfoot (Peat-Cutter of Khiln Moss).

**Places in canon:** Eorlhall, Khiln Moss, Hæringhythe, The Bone-Moot, The Barrow-Downs, The Neck, The Hart-Holt, Hwæl-Loch, Heronstead.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The Bone-Wrights: Carver’s Hand, Comb-Wright, Amulet-Carver, Heirloom-Carver, Master of the Moot
- The Hearth-Troop: Hall-Guard, Shield-Bearer, Thane, Hearth-Thane, Ealdor’s Champion
- The Smokehouses: Gutter, Salter, Smoke-Master, Herring-Reeve

**Faiths with a site here:** Torrad, Leima and Shure.

**Food in canon:** Praen Heather-Mead, Hæringhythe Kipper, Khiln Eel-Pot, Hæringhythe Kippers.

**Garments in canon:** Antler Comb, Horned Amulet, Praen Wolfskin Cloak.

**Rules for new names.** Old-tongue given name + a patronymic in `’s-son` / `’s-daughter` or a hyphened byname. No family names. Keep æ where the root has it. Do not reuse Eorlhall or Heronstead.

## The Knuckle

> “The frozen north of the crooked finger, where it bends east” — `realm_knuckle`

**Sound and patterns.** The Witch-Queens’ north. The sisters have one harsh syllable each: Hrae, Wolk, Skeg, Brund, Yrr, Daeg, Thrin. Their subjects are named as in the Mearc (Hunfrith Tally-Hand, Cenhelm Wada’s-son) or in a seal-coast form (Orri Hreinsson, Ottar Seal-Father).

**People in canon:** Hrae (Eldest of the Striga), Wolk (Krona of the Frost), Skeg (Krona of the Larder), Brund (Krona of the Tithe), Yrr (Krona of the Veil), Daeg (Krona of the Hearing), Thrin (Horned Sister of the Hearing, Keeper of Waerstead), Hunfrith Tally-Hand (Chief Reeve of the Tithe-Road), Ottar Seal-Father (Headman of Ice-Bay), Sister Haeth (Veiled Sister of the Hearing), Ketta Ness (Ice-Fisher of Ice-Bay), Cenhelm Wada’s-son (Tithe-Reeve of the Tithe-Road), Orri Hreinsson (Reindeer-Herder of Hreinmark).

**Places in canon:** The Tithe-Road, Wocca, The Hornhall, The White Waste, Ice-Bay, Waerstead, Hreinmark, The Hush-Wood.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The Tithe-Reeves: Road-Hand, Tithe-Counter, Reeve of the Road, Reeve of the Tithe-Houses, High Reeve
- Outsider-Envoys of the Hearing: Waerstead Interpreter, Bearer of Words, Listener for the Hearing, Outsider-Envoy, Envoy to the Freeholds
- Wardens of the Larder: Ice-Cutter, Store-Guard, Keeper of the Ice, Warden of the Stores

**Faiths with a site here:** Torrad, Leima and Shure.

**Food in canon:** Ice-Bay Seal Stew, Crowberry Spirit.

**Garments in canon:** Reindeer Parka, Bone Snow-Goggles, Tithe-Reeve’s Horn Token.

**Rules for new names.** A new witch is a large change: ask first, and the player never learns their magic. For subjects, use old-tongue names with a byname or patronymic. Do not reuse Wocca or Hreinmark.
