# Polygoetia · culture guide

**Status: draft, waiting for the owner's approval.** See [the Seven's overview](the-seven.md) for how to read a guide.

Sixteen islands inside the Ring, each with its own people and nearly each with its own magic. Written from the Polygoetia reference (`content/canon/polygoetia/`).

## What canon gives, and what it does not

- **No person in Polygoetia has a personal name in canon.** The reference names offices only: the Warden, the Spear-Regent, the Sun-Born, the Rider of the Deep, the Lamp-Queen, the Commodore, the Wing-Marshal, the Rector, the Great Choir, the Harbour-Mothers. Naming anyone here is new lore.
- **The reference's sections on magic schools, faiths and open questions are unwritten.** Schools below are names only. Ask before inventing a Polygoetian school or faith.
- **The Ring is never explained.** Each people has its own account, and none is confirmed.

## Naming, as far as canon shows

- **Islands** are either one soft, open word (Kelumbra, Orivaka, Oloa, Ruhiva, Ascara, Ulumere, Miravel, Puruvala) or plain speech (Brightwater Key, the Wandering Shoal, Freewater, Stonerise, Hushwater, the Great Reef, Mother Smoke, the Eye-Rock). The soft names are heavy with vowels and end in -a, -e or -l.
- **Towns** follow the island's sound: Lumbaran on Kelumbra, Halcyra on Orivaka.
- **Offices and peoples** are hyphened compounds, as everywhere in Allora: Sun-Born, Spear-Regent, Harbour-Mothers, Island-Raisers, Wing-Marshal, Lamp-Queen.
- **Schools and Weathers** are two plain words: Salt-Luck, Kin-Ink, Sky-Sailing, Coral-Song; Gold Calm, Salt Gale, Green Rain, Glass Fog.

**Rules for new names.** A new islet takes plain speech unless it belongs to one of the soft-named islands. Give people an office before a name; when a personal name is needed, build it from the island's own sounds and log it as an addition. Do not reuse any island, school or Weather name.

## The Sunward Chain

### Brightwater Key (levels 70–74)

> “The Harbour-Mothers: matriarchs who own every stall, scale and mooring in the islands' great market port, and settle disputes over tea in the weigh-house” — `pg_brightwater_key`

**Places in canon:** The weigh-house, The lantern-boat harbour, The Callers' pilot-hall, The night market.

**Powers seated here:** The Harbour-Mothers (ruled by: A council of market matriarchs).

**Magic:** none of its own.

### The Wandering Shoal (levels 70–76)

> “The Tuneri: titled families whose orators speak for them with staff and fly-whisk” — `pg_wandering_shoal`

**Places in canon:** The high dunes and their open round-houses, The caravan-grounds, The well of three cups, The shifting sands.

**Powers seated here:** The Tuneri families (ruled by: Titled family heads, speaking through orators).

**Magic:** none of its own.

### Kelumbra (levels 72–80)

> “The Lamplit Kingdom: spice terraces under a volcano, gong-halls, wax-dyed cloth” — `pg_kelumbra`

**Places in canon:** Lumbaran (the queen's city), The lamp-theatres, The clove and nutmeg gardens.

**Powers seated here:** The Lamplit Kingdom (ruled by: The Lamp-Queen, through her plays).

**Magic:** The Lamp-Plays.

### Orivaka (levels 70–92)

> “A kingdom of sworn houses: the sacred Sun-Born reign, the Spear-Regent rules” — `pg_orivaka`

**Places in canon:** The Regent's hill-castle, The great meeting-house, Halcyra and its three colleges, The student quarter.

**Powers seated here:** The Kingdom of Orivaka (ruled by: The Sun-Born reign; the Spear-Regent rules, through sworn houses of hill-fort lords); The University of the Four (ruled by: A Rector and the three college masters, self-governing under the Charter of the Four).

**Magic:** The Fourfold Art (at Halcyra).

### Freewater (levels 74–82)

> “The Articled Court: a pirate republic on an atoll ring, with elections, prize trials and duels at dawn” — `pg_freewater`

**Places in canon:** The Court Hall and the Articles Stone, Gallows Cay, The careening beach, The Graveyard of Ships market.

**Powers seated here:** The Articled Court (ruled by: A Commodore elected each year by the captains).

**Magic:** Salt-Luck.

### Oloa (levels 74–82)

> “The Wayfinders: canoe-houses, stilt villages over lagoons, voyages tattooed on the skin, earth-oven feasts and wave-riding” — `pg_oloa`

**Places in canon:** The canoe-yards, Star-Path Atoll school, The breadfruit groves.

**Powers seated here:** Oloa's canoe-houses (ruled by: A council of navigator-chiefs).

**Magic:** Wayfinding.

### Ruhiva (levels 76–84)

> “The kin-chiefs: ridge-forts, breadfruit valleys, kinship sworn in blood-ink, feuds settled by ink-contests and wrestling” — `pg_ruhiva`

**Places in canon:** The ridge-forts, The inking-house, The contest ground.

**Powers seated here:** The Kin of Ruhiva (ruled by: Feuding kin-chiefs bound by blood-ink).

**Magic:** Kin-Ink.

### Ascara (levels 78–84)

> “The Wing Republic: cliff-clans who govern as one Gathering of everyone who has flown” — `pg_ascara`

**Places in canon:** The launch-cliffs, The wing-lofts, The Gathering Ground, The fire-jar works.

**Powers seated here:** The Wing Republic of Ascara (ruled by: The Gathering of everyone who has flown, and a Wing-Marshal it elects every three years).

**Magic:** Sky-Sailing.

## The Heart-Sea

### Ulumere (levels 82–90)

> “The Tempest Callers: a cloud-wrapped mountain of waterfalls, ruled by shell-choirs of storm-singers” — `pg_ulumere`

**Places in canon:** The choir-terraces, The conch reef, The falls.

**Powers seated here:** The Tempest Callers (ruled by: The Great Choir: the eldest singers, mostly women).

**Magic:** Tempest Calling.

### Stonerise (levels 82–90)

> “The Island-Raisers: a basalt island raised from the sea within living memory” — `pg_stonerise`

**Places in canon:** The basalt stair, The raising-grounds, The land-registry.

**Powers seated here:** The Raisers' Guild (ruled by: Guild-masters by contract).

**Magic:** Island-Raising.

### Hushwater (levels 84–92)

> “The Deep Quiet: an atoll lagoon of perfect calm, where silent monks dive for pearls and speak mind to mind” — `pg_hushwater`

**Places in canon:** The still lagoon, The diving-steps, The cells of listening.

**Powers seated here:** Hushwater (ruled by: Nobody speaks; the eldest mind decides).

**Magic:** The Deep Quiet.

### The Great Reef (levels 84–92)

> “The coral-singers: a city half on and half under the reef, of turtles, mantas and giant-clam gardens” — `pg_great_reef`

**Places in canon:** The clam gardens, Manta Pass, The singing caves.

**Magic:** Coral-Song.

### Mother Smoke (levels 84–92)

> “The volcano: fire-hermits, dragon-lizards, obsidian and sulphur” — `pg_mother_smoke`

**Places in canon:** The crater lip, The lizard slopes, The obsidian fields.

**Magic:** none of its own (a faith).

### Miravel (levels 86–95)

> “Half an island” — `pg_miravel`

**Places in canon:** The Warden's library-tower, The singing orchards, The revel-glade (seen in Glass Fog), The Spring Under the Hill.

**Powers seated here:** Miravel (ruled by: The Warden over the mortal half; the Glimmering Court over the hidden half).

**Magic:** Revel-Craft.

### Puruvala (levels 88–95)

> “The Whale-Mandala: a sea-empire whose temple-mountain capital stands on the back of the Great Whale, a creature the size of an island that has slept for centuries” — `pg_puruvala`

**Places in canon:** The temple-mountain and its mandala terraces, The spout-fountains, The royal barge-harbour, The Whale's Ear (a cave where its heartbeat is heard).

**Powers seated here:** The Whale-Mandala of Puruvala (ruled by: The Rider of the Deep and a court of poets and admirals).

**Magic:** The Whale-Mandala.

### The Eye-Rock (levels 90–95)

> “A bare rock at the Ring's inner edge, where the Callers' great choir sings the doors once a year” — `pg_eye_rock`

**Places in canon:** The singing-ledge, The Ringwall.

**Magic:** none of its own.

## The Weathers

Gold Calm, Salt Gale, Green Rain, Glass Fog, Ember Squall, The Long Ebb, Singing Gale (rare), The Eye (rarest). Only the Tempest Callers make weather.
