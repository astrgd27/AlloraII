# The Seven · culture guide (overview)

**Status: draft, waiting for the owner's approval.** Written from canon (`content/canon/allora1/`, extracted from Allora I).

This page covers what the seven realms share. Each realm has its own guide:
[Eintenn](eintenn.md) · [Luggen](luggen.md) · [Grell](grell.md) · [Sarlon](sarlon.md) · [Dellian](dellian.md) · [Rolla](rolla.md) · [Marre](marre.md).

## How to read these guides

- A line starting with `>` is canon, quoted word for word, followed by the id of the canon entry it comes from. A test (`guides.test.ts`) checks every quote against canon.
- Lists of names, ranks and dishes are canon names, copied from the extracted files.
- **"Canon is silent"** means I searched and found nothing. Anything under **Proposed** is mine, not canon: it is there for you to accept, change or strike.
- "Rules for new names" are the working rules that follow from the evidence. The content lint already refuses a new name that copies or nearly copies a canon one.

## What the realms share

**The Reckoning and the old tongue.**

> “Years in the Seven are counted Anno Ruinae — from the fall of Vaelis and the Allorese Empire with it.” — `lore_reckoning`

> “Scholars still date their letters in High Allorese, and the Dellian Diet keeps its records in it, so that no living principality’s tongue is favoured.” — `lore_reckoning`

**The Diet.** Each realm sends electors to Dellian, which keeps the peace by being needed.

> “the Arch-Bishop presides over the Dellian Diet, to which each of the Seven sends electors to decide common policy and resolve disputes” — `realm_dellian`

**Three great powers and their shadows.** Sarlon, Dellian and Marre are the great powers. Grell lies in Sarlon's shadow and Rolla under Marre's.

> “The Duchy of Grell lies in its shadow.” — `realm_sarlon`

> “The Kingdom of Rolla lies under its influence.” — `realm_marre`

**Shared sights.** The same things turn up in town after town across the Seven, under the same names: Pecutto's Cart and Pecutto's Players' Wagons, the Travelling Carnival, the Pest-Houses, the Royal Progress, and a Dellian Lamp-Chapel.

> “A barrel on a painted cart, a priest who has clearly been sampling, and a crowd that grows by the hour.” — `ostwick/pecart`

**Money.** The coin is the pfennig, written "pf".

> “the Barley Sheaf serves ale to anyone with a pfennig” — `ostwick`

**Faiths cross borders.** A god has a home realm and followers elsewhere. Dell has lamp-chapels in every court; Saint Calder has a chapel at Burg Tennfels and a reliquary in Marre; Horro's Mother-Hall is in Luggen and his followers bank in Marre.

## Rules for new names, anywhere in the Seven

1. **Follow the realm's sound.** Each realm guide lists canon names to pattern on. A Sarlonese count is not called Hartwig; a Luggish reeve is not called Gonzalo.
2. **Never reuse or near-copy a canon name.** The lint enforces this for places, people, groups, faiths and schools.
3. **Plain-speech names are safe everywhere.** Canon is full of them: The Barley Sheaf, The Gilded Eel, The Stone Mole, The Herring Quay, The Wolf's Gate. A new inn, quay or gate can always be named this way.
4. **Compound nouns with a hyphen are the house style** for offices and things: Salt-Duke, Mask-Warden, Lamp-Guard, Dike-Count, Hearth-Mother, Loop-Lord, Drove-Road.
5. **No real-world historical terms** in game text (a world rule). The realms echo real places in their sounds only.

## Where canon is silent, across the Seven

- **Dress.** Canon describes clothing only where it means something: mourning black in Grell, yellow-and-white livery in Dellian, a cloak of pheasant feathers in Rolla. There is no general account of what people wear in any realm.
- **Oaths and sayings.** Canon has oaths as institutions (Saint Calder, the Lamp-Guard's oath, the Cave-Sworn) but almost no everyday oaths, curses or proverbs.
- **Family names of ordinary people** are few: a handful per realm, from the people a player can befriend.

Each realm guide says what it found and proposes how to fill the gap.
