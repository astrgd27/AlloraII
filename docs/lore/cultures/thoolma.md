# Thoolma · culture guide

**Status: draft, waiting for the owner's approval.** See [the Seven's overview](the-seven.md) for how to read a guide.

The East: the empire of Plandor, its client kingdoms and the tribes of the Raizon, all under the Hum. Names in the city are long and formal; they shorten the further you go from it.

This guide gives, for each realm, its canon names, ranks, faiths, food and garments, and the naming rules that follow. The lists are copied from canon by script. It does not yet propose dress or oaths where canon is silent; that waits until a phase builds in this land.

## Plandor of the Crescent Bay

> “Plandor is the first megalopolis of Allora: four million people living under gold and glass domes on the hills that ring the Narrows and the Crescent Bay” — `realm_plandor`

**Sound and patterns.** Imperial names are a given name + a long family name: Orrin Vasilant, Theoda Marcellin, Corvan Ilarion, Hebe Sarrantine, Aurel Quiro. Office comes first: Minister, Lady, Prince. Street people have bynames: Big Tullo, Old Mother Crab. A citizen is `of` a ward.

**People in canon:** Stefos Quinallia, the Wind-Up Emperor (Sovereign of the Key), Orrin Vasilant (Keeper of the Key), Proctus Ammar (First Minister of Petitions), Ysolt Brask (Grand Winder of the Guild), Hebe Sarrantine (Mistress of Colour), Anatole Vey (First Copyist of the Exemplar), Old Mother Crab (Matriarch of the Crab-Teams), Theoda Marcellin (Copyist of the Exemplar), Corvan Ilarion (Charioteer of Verdigris), Nephele Saro (Journeywoman of the Winders’ Guild), Dasko Halar (Toll-Poet of the Long Stair Skyway), Lady Ismene Orestal (Daughter of a Keyward House), Prince Haverun of Seylon (The Honoured Guest), Minister Aurel Quiro (Eunuch-Minister of Petitions), Big Tullo (Boss of the Biggest Crab-Team).

**Places in canon:** The Narrows Harbour, The Imperial Quarter, The Palace of Faces, The Varndum Yards, The Dome of the Exemplar, The Bowl, The Nine Ministries, The Crescent Reefs, The Mint of Colour, The Cistern Canals, The Hanging Wards, The Garden of Enclosed Climates, The Crystal Exchange, The Round Courts, The Bark Hospice, The Triple Walls, The Columbarium Skyway.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The Winders’ Guild: Apprentice of the Escapement, Spring-Tempering Journeyman, Warrant-Copyist, Master Winder of a Rank, Warden of the Thousand
- The Nine Ministries: Copy-Clerk of Notices, Reader of Petitions, Secretary of a Form, Under-Minister, Minister of a Dome
- The Factions of the Bowl: Stable-Hand of the Wheel, Reserve Driver, Driver of a Colour, First Driver, Champion of the Bowl
- The Copyists of the Exemplar: Novice of the Screen, Copyist of Icons, Copyist of the Nave, Elder Copyist, Hand of the First Copyist

**Faiths with a site here:** The Exemplar, Ximarro, the Hinge, Tessaloe, the Kiln, Haranqua, the Maw, Vespiro, the Unreckoned.

**Food in canon:** Ward Sherbet, Ward Loaf, Ward-Smoked Blind Carp, Pickled Giant-Fruit Rind, Jar of the Nine Wards.

**Garments in canon:** Indoor Parasol, Copyist’s White, Minister’s Seal-Ring, Winder’s Brass Key, Charioteer’s Bronze Cap, Cinnabar Scarf, Verdigris Scarf, Lapis Scarf, Orpiment Scarf.

**Rules for new names.** Classical-sounding given name + three-syllable family name; give the ward. Do not reuse Quinallia or Vasilant. The Emperor is one man: never add another.

## Therama, the Hill-Country

> “Therama is the hill-country that keeps Plandor alive: grain terraces, silk farms, golem foundries and the head of the great aqueduct, all of it owned by city money” — `realm_therama`

**Sound and patterns.** Hill-country names are shorter: Ilka Varr, Odrin Kamen, Ennodo Vash. Bynames tell a story: Tavi Long-Step, Oru Who Is Not There. Places are a name + what is there: Saltrou of the Mulberries.

**People in canon:** Foundress Ilka Varr (Mistress of the Vel Ossira Foundries), Odrin Kamen (Terrace-Farmer of Kamenara), Saba of the Dovecotes (Heir of a Dovecote Family), Clerk Ennodo Vash (Clerk of the Ombudsman), Oru Who Is Not There (Elder of the Shade-Lodges), Tavi Long-Step (Path-Keeper of the Vessi).

**Places in canon:** Kamenara, the Great Hive, Vel Ossira, the Foundries, Doroskene and the Thirsty Arch, Saltrou of the Mulberries, The Relay Stairs, The Edge of the Trees, Velmira of the Orchard-Cones, The Under-Canopy.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The Lease Hall of Vel Ossira: Lease-Runner, Inspector of Golems, Field-Golem Leaser, Keeper of the Lease-Cords, Lease-Master of Therama

**Rules for new names.** Two-syllable given name + one-syllable family name, or a byname. Do not reuse Kamenara or Vel Ossira.

## The Kingdom of Seylon

> “Seylon is the oldest kingdom in the East and the most thoroughly owned” — `realm_seylon`

**Sound and patterns.** Mountain names: Anahid, Tazmir, Ani Khazaran, Gorvan. Kings take a number and a byname (Gorvan IV, the Gilded Guest). Ministers have Plandor names, because they are from Plandor.

**People in canon:** Gorvan IV, the Gilded Guest (King of Seylon), Minister Caspar Velloe (Resident of the Key in Seylon), Mother Anahid of the Lamp (Abbess of the Monastery of the Lamp), Ani Khazaran (Cord-Reader of the Valley of Cords), Old Tazmir (Keeper of the Old Dorum).

**Places in canon:** Khaderun, the Crater City, The Lapidary Houses, The Singing Mines of the Glass Peaks, The Monastery of the Lamp, The Old Dorum of Khaderun, The Valley of Cords, The High Pass Pastures.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The Lapidary Houses: Sweeper of the Wheel-Room, Apprentice of the Clear Cut, Journeyman of the Veiled Cut, Master of the Deep Cut, Master of All Three Cuts
- The Dorum-Keepers: Lamp-Trimmer, Dealer of the Side-Tables, Reader of Debts, Dealer of the High Table, Keeper of the Dorum

**Faiths with a site here:** The Lamp in the Stone.

**Food in canon:** Seylon Apricots in Brandy.

**Rules for new names.** Seylonese names with kh, z, v sounds for natives; Plandor names for the Resident’s people. Do not reuse Khaderun.

## Gramanna, the Kingdom of Bells

> “Gramanna is a loud, hot, river-born kingdom whose people live on boats as much as on land” — `realm_gramanna`

**Sound and patterns.** River names, bright and open: Iara Sinoa, Benedo Malha, Jacira, Orsola Venn. Titles are joined to a trade: Queen-Pilot, Grand Master. Rivers have names: the Negarro, the Lumbre.

**People in canon:** Grand Master Orsola Venn (Grand Master of the Clean Stroke), Iara Sinoa (River-Pilot of the Lodge), Benedo Malha (Journeyman of the Clean Stroke), Queen-Pilot Jacira (Queen of Gramanna).

**Places in canon:** Campanara, the Floating City, The Bell-Foundries, The Pilots’ Lodge, The House of the Clean Stroke, The Black Banks of the Negarro, The Gold Orchards of the Lumbre, The Lantern Reach of the Lumbre.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The Pilots’ Lodge: Line-Hand, Sounder, River-Pilot, Fleet-Pilot, Elder of the Lodge
- The Guild of the Clean Stroke: Keeper of the One Bell, Copyist of Last Words, Journeyman in Ash, Assize-Warden, Master in Slate

**Faiths with a site here:** The Mother of Bells.

**Food in canon:** Glowfish Broth, Negarro Ice-Ham, Ice-Ham of the Fourth Form.

**Rules for new names.** Vowel-ending given and family names. Bells, boats and rivers for places. Do not reuse Campanara.

## Topila, the Kingdom of Tables

> “Topila is a land of sheer-sided table mountains rising out of the jungle into cloud” — `realm_topila`

**Sound and patterns.** Few names in canon: Seferin Oll, Ilari Quen, Tevo Arrasol. Soft given names, short or flowing family names. Places are Tables: the High Table, the Ash Table; the jungle is the Floor.

**People in canon:** Abbot-Laureate Seferin Oll (Ruler of Topila), Ilari Quen (Poet of the Misremembering School), Tevo Arrasol (Sun-Smith of the Mirror Table).

**Places in canon:** The High Table, The Table of Mirrors, The Rime Table, The Floor, The Banner-Ropes, The Table of the Burning Hand, The Ash Table.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The Sun-Smiths of the Mirror Table: Mirror-Polisher, Frame-Turner, Glass-Fuser, Blade-Temperer, Master of Noon

**Faiths with a site here:** Iru, the Listening Sun.

**Rules for new names.** Follow the three canon names. A new mesa is `The … Table`. Canon is thin here: propose little.

## The Southern Tribes

> “In the deep Raizon, Thoolma rules by paying people” — `realm_south`

**Sound and patterns.** Many peoples, so many forms: titles as names (the Nodarius, Nodaria Vel), bynames (Kaia Tall-Step, Ruro of the Square, Amaro of the Figs), and a king numbered by year (Pell the Seventh-Year).

**People in canon:** King Pell the Seventh-Year (Child King of Dapilla), Prefect Lucan Ardesh (Prefect of the Stipend Stone), The Nodarius of the Teira (Master of the Velatores), Nodaria Vel (Velator of the Greza, between contracts), Amaro of the Figs (Elder of Yesterday, once King of Dapilla), Kaia Tall-Step (Kilopa Archer of Old Corolla), Ruro of the Square (Gualdara Fisherman), Sister Mallow (Frizata Healer).

**Places in canon:** The Stipend Stone, The Mist Gorges of the Teira, The Orchard Kingdom of Dapilla, Old Corolla, the Walking Village, The Camps of the Ayermar, The Monasteries of the Ondoro.

**Ranks in canon:**
- The Velatores: Filum, a Thread, Velator of One Knot, Velator, Weaver of Contracts, Nodarius-in-Waiting
- The Frizata: Gate-Novice, Gardener of Simples, Healer of the Round Infirmary, Keeper of a Door, Elder of the Four Doors
- The Stipend Office: Stipend-Clerk, Interpreter of the Stone, Patrol-Captain of the Drone, Deputy Prefect, Prefect of a Stone

**Faiths with a site here:** The Dreaming Child, The Five Rivers.

**Food in canon:** Shell-Boar in Leaves, Leaf-Wrapped Shell-Boar Ham, Fermented Dream-Fruit, Deep-Fermented Dream-Fruit.

**Rules for new names.** Name the tribe first, then follow its one or two canon examples. Bynames are safe.

## The Southern Reach

> “The Southern Reach is the vacated heart of Anquerra: whole cities closed up, tidied and left, with a key hanging on a nail beside every door and a notice of vacancy above it” — `realm_reach`

**Sound and patterns.** Almost empty, and meant to be. Two people in canon: Old Steward Ammaru, Anquel. The Lease is never explained.

**People in canon:** Old Steward Ammaru (Eldest of the Holdovers), Anquel (A Holdover of the Southern Reach).

**Places in canon:** Oruvande, the Counting City, The Stair of Seven Hundred, The Last Gate, The Throat, The Grazing.

**Rules for new names.** Add people sparingly. Never say why Anquerra left or what the Lease was.
